Privacy Policy

How we handle your personal and order information at Ducor International Pharmacy.

Last updated: September 2026 · Ducor International Pharmacy · Affiliate of PharMed Consultants Liberia, INC

Who we are

Ducor International Pharmacy (“we”, “us”) operates ducor-international-pharmacy.com from Monrovia, Liberia. We sell prescription medications, over-the-counter medicines, and vitamins online and in person.

Information we collect

  • Account & contact details — name, email, phone, and delivery or pickup details you provide at checkout or when creating an account.
  • Order information — products ordered, prescription-related details you upload or share with our pharmacists, payment method chosen, and order status.
  • Payment references — for MTN Mobile Money, Sendwave, bank transfer, or cash on delivery/pickup, we record what you send so we can match payment to your order. We do not store full bank login credentials or card PINs on this website.
  • Technical data — basic analytics (for example Google Analytics) such as pages visited and device type, to improve the site.

How we use your information

  • Process, verify, and fulfill orders (pickup or delivery).
  • Contact you by phone, WhatsApp, or email about payment confirmation, prescription review, or delivery.
  • Provide pharmacist support for prescription products — we do not give medical advice through this site alone; prescriptions are reviewed by a pharmacist.
  • Improve our website and customer service, and meet legal or regulatory obligations for pharmacy operations in Liberia.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with:

  • Staff and affiliated operators of PharMed Consultants Liberia, INC as needed to fill and deliver your order.
  • Payment networks you choose (for example MTN, Sendwave, or your bank) when you send payment.
  • Service providers that host this site or analytics, under reasonable safeguards.
  • Authorities when required by Liberian law.

Retention & security

We keep order and account records as long as needed for pharmacy operations, customer support, and legal requirements, then delete or anonymize them when no longer needed. We use standard technical and organizational measures to protect data; no online transmission is 100% secure.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you (subject to pharmacy record-keeping rules) by emailing dipmonrovia@gmail.com or messaging us on WhatsApp. You can also ask us to stop marketing messages at any time.
